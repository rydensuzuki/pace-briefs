01Volume & load · four weeks

13.8 hours total, up from 12.1h the week before, continuing a four-week climb in hours: 9.9 -> 10.1 -> 12.1 -> 13.8. Load actually eased slightly though, 908 -> 831, the first dip in load despite more time on feet and in the saddle — average intensity per hour came down a notch. Bike carried the most volume again, 7.3h for 312 load, roughly matching last week's 6.8h/339. Run hours held flat at 4.2h but load dropped from 504 to 414. Swim hours rose (2.3h vs 1.4h) but stayed at just 2 sessions, load 105.

Monday's 16-mile long run was the headline: a genuine negative split, pace dropping from 11:06/mi to 10:19/mi as HR climbed from 134 to 147, but the average intensity (IF 99.6) was essentially threshold effort sustained for 2h40m — actual load 258 against a planned 158, on a day TSB read -40. Wednesday's "easy" Z2 ride told a different story: only IF 62, but power faded 8% across the ride (97W down to 89W) with about 9% aerobic decoupling — soft output relative to HR for a supposedly recovery-pace spin. Friday's 4h45m long ride, by contrast, was clean: essentially flat power and HR across all four quartiles, 0.2% fade at a conservative IF 62.5 — good aerobic control at the week's biggest single session. The brick run right off that ride showed the bill coming due fast: just 20 minutes, but HR climbed from 132 to 152 at a near-constant pace, about 11% decoupling.
